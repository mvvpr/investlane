Methodology

How we rank stock apps

Every fact comes from the broker's own website or its app store listings, and every review links its sources.

Two rankings, two audiences

Each app gets an editorial score out of 5 for each audience, and the list re-orders when you switch.

  • New investors: commission-free core trading, low currency and account fees, low minimums, and app rating and review volume.
  • Experienced traders: number of markets and asset classes, options and futures access, cost per trade at size, and platform depth.
